Four depths. Boule writes none of the first three — they are the government's own text. Every layer names its source, and the full text is always one link away.

L0 What it does one line

Requires federal agencies to publish a plain-language notice before collecting personal data, and gives individuals a right to correct it.

L1 Official summary CRS · at House passage

This bill establishes a notice-and-correction framework for personal data held by federal agencies. Each agency must publish a plain-language notice describing what it collects, why, and for how long it is retained.

Individuals may request correction of inaccurate records, and agencies must respond within a fixed period.

It does not create a private right of action, and it does not apply to state or private-sector data.
